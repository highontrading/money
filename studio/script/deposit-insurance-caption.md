# DICGC deposit insurance · Instagram caption

Bank doob gaya toh ₹20 lakh FD mein se kitna milega? Guarantee sirf ₹5 lakh ki. 🏦

Is reel mein:
• DICGC: har depositor, har bank, ₹5 lakh tak (~90 din mein)
• Interest bhi isi ₹5 lakh mein
• Limit per bank hai, branch ya account ki alag nahi
• NBFC / company FD, mutual fund covered nahi
• Bachao kaise: alag alag bank, har ek mein ₹5 lakh se thoda kam

Jo apni saari savings ek hi bank mein rakhta hai, use bhejo 📲

Educational only · not financial advice. DICGC cover ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank since Feb 2020 (₹1 lakh earlier); limit as of Oct 2026. Check dicgc.org.in for the insured banks list.
