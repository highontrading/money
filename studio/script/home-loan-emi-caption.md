# Home loan EMI · Instagram caption

Pehle saal ₹5.2 lakh EMI bhari… aur loan ghata sirf ₹1 lakh? 🏠😳

Is reel mein:
• Har EMI ke do hisse: interest vs principal
• 10 saal baad bhi ₹35 lakh (70%) baaki
• ₹50 lakh loan = ₹1.04 crore total, ₹54 lakh sirf interest
• Rate badha toh EMI nahi, tenure badhta hai (+27 mahine)
• ₹1 lakh/saal prepayment: 14 saal mein khatam, ~₹18.5 lakh bachat
