# New vs old tax regime (80C) · Instagram caption

₹1.5 lakh ki "tax-saving" policy li… new regime mein ek rupaya nahi bacha? 🧾

Is reel mein:
• New regime default hai, aur usme 80C wali deductions nahi
• ₹12 lakh salary: new regime mein tax zero (₹75,000 standard deduction + ₹12 lakh tak rebate)
• Old regime mein, 80C ke saath bhi ~₹1.17 lakh
• Old regime kab jeetta hai: bade deductions (HRA, home loan interest…)
• Pehle regime chuno, phir investment

Jo dost March mein tax bachane ki policy dhoondhta hai, use bhejo 📲
