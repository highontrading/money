# Closing a credit card · Instagram caption

Purana credit card band kiya… aur CIBIL score gir gaya? 📉

Is reel mein:
• Credit utilization: limit ka kitna hissa use hota hai
• ₹60,000 kharcha: ₹2 lakh limit pe 30%, ₹1 lakh pe 60%
• Purana card = lambi credit history
• Kya karein: free card band mat karo, lifetime-free maango, limit badhwao

Jo dost apna purana card band karne wala hai, use bhejo 📲

Educational only · not financial advice. "Under 30%" is a common guideline, not an official cutoff; credit bureaus don't publish exact factor weights.
