# Popcorn pricing · Instagram caption

Ticket ₹280, popcorn ₹400? 🍿 Yeh lalach nahi, business model hai.

Is reel mein:
• Ticket ka bada hissa film walon ko jaata hai
• Popcorn ka paisa poora theatre ka, margin bhi bada
• Har darshak avg ~₹150 ka khaana
• Decoy pricing: medium isliye hai taaki large sasta lage
• Free peene ka paani aapka haq

Jo dost har movie pe large combo leta hai, use bhejo 📲
