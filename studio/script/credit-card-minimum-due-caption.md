# Credit card minimum due · Instagram caption

Minimum due time pe bhara. Late fee bhi nahi lagi. Phir ₹3,244 ka interest? 😳

Is reel mein:
• Interest-free period kab milta hai, kab chhin jaata hai
• Interest poore bill pe, shopping ke din se
• ₹50,000 ka asli hisaab: ₹2,130 + ₹618 + 18% GST
• Sirf minimum bharte raho toh 20+ saal, ₹2.5 lakh+
• Karna kya hai: poora bill, auto-debit on total due
