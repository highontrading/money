Status: **script draft for approval.** No voice credits spent yet.

## The idea

An evergreen video that teaches the full IPO journey to anyone, whether a first-time investor, their parents or their grandparents. It uses two threads:

- **Raju ki Chai**, a made-up chai company. It explains every mechanism (why a company needs partners, fresh issue vs OFS, the cake split) without ever going out of date.
- **Jio Platforms (Oct 2026)** as the real worked example: price band, lot, anchors, subscription, allotment maths. Jio is only ever "the example", never "the news", so the video doesn't expire after listing day. The script never states Jio's listing-day result.

Bookends: **Reliance 1977** (₹10 share, 58,000 investors, paper forms) → **Jio 2026** (₹30,213 crore, UPI). "Paisa badla, technology badli, par raasta wahi hai." That gives every generation a way in: grandparents remember forms and certificates, parents remember refund cheques, the young audience knows UPI.

## Length

The voice-over runs about 10.5–12 min depending on pace, plus ~30 s of chapter stings and the end screen, so roughly **11–12 min total**. The cold-open test read will give the exact pace. If you'd like it nearer 10 min, the easiest cuts are C3 (the profit/loss quota nuance, 2 lines) and C1 (angel/VC line, the "do cheezein" setup), about 45 s together.
