# Direct vs regular plan · Instagram caption

Same fund. Same SIP. 25 saal baad ₹31 lakh ka farak? 😮
Wajah: ek chhota sa 1%.

Is reel mein:
• Har fund ke do plan: Regular vs Direct
• Expense ratio aur distributor commission
• 1% ka compounding: 5 saal ₹22,000 → 25 saal ₹31 lakh
• Apna plan kaise check karein (fund ka naam, CAS)
• Switch se pehle: exit load aur capital gains tax

Jiska SIP bank wale bhaiya ne khulwaya hai, use bhejo 📲

Educational only · not investment advice. Illustrative: ₹10,000 monthly SIP, 12% vs 11% a year. Direct plans exist since 1 Jan 2013 (SEBI). Regular-vs-direct expense gap is typically ~0.5–1.5% for equity funds.
