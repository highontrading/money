# PF withdrawal on job switch · Instagram caption

Job badli, PF ke ₹3 lakh nikaal liye… aur retirement ke ₹32 lakh chale gaye? 😬

Is reel mein:
• EPF ka 8.25% compounding: ₹3 lakh → 30 saal mein ~₹32 lakh
• Har job switch pe nikaalo toh compounding zero se shuru
• 5 saal se pehle nikaala: taxable + TDS ho sakta hai
• Sahi tareeka: UAN se online transfer
• Emergency? Poora nahi, zaroorat jitna

Jisne abhi abhi resign kiya hai, use bhejo 📲

Educational only · not financial advice. Illustrative: ₹3 lakh at 8.25% (EPF rate for FY 2025-26) for 30 years, no new contributions; future rates may differ.
