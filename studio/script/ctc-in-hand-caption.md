# CTC vs in-hand · Instagram caption

Offer letter mein ₹12 lakh, pehli salary ₹79,000? 🧾 ₹21,000 kahan gaye?

Is reel mein:
• CTC = Cost to Company, aapki salary nahi
• Variable, employer PF, gratuity, insurance ke tukde
• Aapka PF + professional tax kat ke in-hand
• Zyaadatar paisa aapka hi hai (PF = retirement)
• Offer compare karo in-hand se, breakup maango

Jisne abhi abhi offer letter paaya hai, use bhejo 📲

Educational only · not financial advice. Illustrative ₹12 lakh CTC; new tax regime FY 2026-27; professional tax varies by state. Your breakup will differ.
