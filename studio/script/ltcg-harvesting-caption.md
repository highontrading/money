Har saal sarkar ek ₹15,625 ka tax-free coupon deti hai. Aap use kar rahe ho? 🎟️

Is reel mein:
• Equity LTCG: pehle ₹1.25 lakh/saal tax-free, upar 12.5%
• Chhoot saal dar saal judti nahi
• Harvesting: profit book karo, turant reinvest, kharid keemat upar
• 10 saal mein ₹1.5 lakh+ tax bachat
• Dhyan: 1 saal purane units, exit load, stocks + funds ki combined limit, March se pehle
