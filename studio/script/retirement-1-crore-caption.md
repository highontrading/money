# ₹1 crore retirement · Instagram caption

₹1 crore jodo aur retire ho jao? 25 saal baad woh sirf 5 saal chalega. ⏳

Is reel mein:
• 6% mehngai: 25 saal mein daam ~4.3 guna
• ₹1 crore = aaj ke ~₹23 lakh
• ₹50,000/mahina ka kharcha → ₹2.15 lakh/mahina
• 30 saal ke retirement ke liye ~₹6 crore+
• Kya karein: mehngai jod ke target, jaldi shuru, SIP step-up, sirf FD nahi

Jo kehta hai ₹1 crore kaafi hai, use bhejo 📲
