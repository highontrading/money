# Health insurance room-rent cap · Instagram caption

₹5 lakh ka cover, ₹3 lakh ka bill… claim mila sirf ₹2.25 lakh? 🏥

Is reel mein:
• Room rent limit (aksar 1% of cover per din)
• Proportionate deduction: room se jude saare kharche 62.5%
• Bill ka hisaab: ₹75,000 jeb se, jabki room ka extra sirf ₹15,000 tha
• IRDAI rule: dawai, implants, tests, ICU pe katauti nahi
• Kya karein: limit ke andar room, ya no-room-rent-limit policy
