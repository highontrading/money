## Rupee near ₹95
*Rupee ₹95: aapki jeb pe asar* · 20 lines · ~1167 chars

> Facts: Rupee closed 94.81/$ on 22 Oct 2026 (PTI); ~88.20 on 30 Oct 2025 (AIR). Record low ~96.97 in May 2026. FPIs pulled ~Rs 3.08 lakh crore from Indian equities in CY26 to mid-Oct (Business Standard, record). Example fees $40,000: Rs 35.3 lakh at 88.2 vs Rs 37.9 lakh at 94.8. Remittance $1,000: Rs 88,200 vs Rs 94,800.

01. Ek saal pehle 1 dollar = **₹88.** Aaj lagbhag **₹95.**
02. Dollar mehenga nahi hua. **Rupee sasta** hua. Aur iska bill aapki jeb tak aata hai.
03. Rupee ka daam bhi **demand aur supply** se tay hota hai.
04. Pehla kaaran: **tel.** Tel mehenga, toh India ko zyada dollar chahiye.
05. Doosra: **videshi investor.** Is saal Indian shares se **₹3 lakh crore** se zyada nikaal chuke hain.
06. Wo rupee bech kar dollar lete hain. Dollar ki demand badhi, **rupee gira.**
07. May mein toh rupee **₹97** ke paas pahunch gaya tha. Ab tak ka sabse neeche.
08. Ab aapki jeb. Bachcha America mein padh raha hai? Fees **$40,000** saal.
09. Pichhle saal yeh lagbhag **₹35 lakh** thi. Aaj **₹38 lakh.** Fees wahi, kharcha zyada.
10. Foreign trip, imported phone, laptop. Sab **mehenga.**
11. Aur tel bhi dollar mein aata hai. Toh **mehngai** ka dabaav alag.
12. Par kuch log **khush** bhi hain.
13. Bahar se ghar paisa bhejne wale. **$1,000** pe pehle ₹88,000 milte the, ab **₹95,000.**
14. Aur **IT companies,** jo dollar mein kamaati hain.
15. RBI kya karta hai? Apne **reserve** se dollar bechta hai, taaki girawat **dheemi** ho.
16. Rokta nahi. Bas **jhatke** kam karta hai.
17. Aap kya karein? Videsh ka bada kharcha aane wala hai, toh dollar **thoda-thoda** lo.
18. Aur portfolio mein thoda **international** hissa, rupee ke girne se kuch bachaav deta hai.
19. Jiska bachcha bahar padh raha hai, usse yeh **bhejo.**

## FD rates after the repo hike
*Repo badha, FD kab badhegi?* · 18 lines · ~1175 chars

> Facts: RBI repo 5.25% -> 5.50% on 7 Oct 2026. ICICI revised FD rates from 13 Oct; most big banks unchanged mid-Oct; SBI chairman: deposit rates may take 2-3 months (Upstox/GoPocket). NBFC FDs up to ~8.95% (Ventura). DICGC cover Rs 5 lakh per depositor per bank incl. interest (since 4 Feb 2020); NBFC deposits not covered.

01. RBI ne repo rate badhaya. **5.25%** se **5.50%.**
02. Toh aapki FD ka rate bhi badha? Zyada tar banks mein, **abhi nahi.**
03. Kyun? Naye loan ka rate repo se **seedha juda** hai. Jaldi badhta hai.
04. Par FD ka rate bank **khud** tay karta hai. Jab deposit ki zaroorat ho, tab badhata hai.
05. Kuch banks ne badhaya hai. Par SBI ke chairman ne kaha: **2–3 mahine** lag sakte hain.
06. Aur idhar NBFC FD: **8.95%** tak! Zyada lagta hai na?
07. Par ek fark samjho. Bank FD pe **DICGC** ka insurance hai.
08. Har bank mein, har depositor ke **₹5 lakh** tak. Bank doobe, toh bhi yeh paisa surakshit.
09. NBFC FD pe yeh insurance **nahi.** Isliye **rating** zaroor dekho.
10. Zyada byaaj matlab **zyada risk.** Yeh rule kabhi mat bhoolna.
11. Toh abhi FD karein ya ruken? Ek tareeka: **laddering.**
12. Maan lo ₹3 lakh. Ek saath nahi. **1 lakh** ek saal, **1 lakh** do saal, **1 lakh** teen saal.
13. Har saal ek FD **mature** hogi. Rate badhe, toh naye rate pe dobara lagao.
14. Rate gire, toh baaki FD **purane rate** pe chal rahi hain.
15. Senior citizen ho, toh aksar **0.5%** zyada milta hai. Poochna mat bhoolo.
16. Aur FD ka byaaj aapki income mein judta hai. **Slab ke hisaab se tax.**
17. Jo roz poochta hai 'FD rate kab badhega', usse yeh **bhejo.**
18. Paisa samjho. Yeh hai **Money Theory.**

## No-cost EMI
*No-cost EMI: cost kahan chhupi hai?* · 19 lines · ~1078 chars

> Facts: Illustrative: Rs 60,000 phone, 6 x Rs 10,000. At ~16% p.a. bank rate, financed principal ~Rs 57,360, interest ~Rs 2,640, given back as upfront discount. GST 18% on interest ~Rs 475. Processing fee typically ~Rs 200 + GST (varies by bank). Interest is shifted to the merchant as a discount, not waived (Business Today, Zerodha Varsity, Jupiter).

01. Phone **₹60,000.** No-cost EMI: **₹10,000** mahina, 6 mahine.
02. Total ₹60,000. Toh free hai na? **Poora nahi.**
03. EMI matlab **loan.** Aur bank free mein loan nahi deta.
04. Toh byaaj kaun bharta hai? Dukaan ya brand, aapko utna **discount** de deta hai.
05. Maan lo lagbhag **₹2,600** ka discount. Aur bank utna hi **byaaj** le leta hai. Hisaab barabar.
06. Ab chhupe kharche. Pehla: byaaj pe **18% GST.**
07. ₹2,600 ke byaaj pe lagbhag **₹470.** Yeh aap bharte ho. Statement mein dikhta hai.
08. Doosra: **processing fee.** Aksar ₹200 ke aas-paas, upar se GST.
09. Teesra, sabse bada: **cash discount.** Poora paisa ek saath do, toh kai baar alag offer milta hai.
10. No-cost EMI chuna, toh woh discount **chhoot** jaata hai.
11. Aur yeh loan aapki **credit report** mein dikhega.
12. Ek EMI chooki, toh late fee, aur **credit score** pe daag.
13. Kai chhote EMI ek saath? Aage **bada loan** lene mein dikkat ho sakti hai.
14. Toh tap karne se pehle **teen sawaal.**
15. Ek: poora paisa dene pe kitna **discount?** Dono ka final daam milao.
16. Do: GST aur fee **milaakar** total kitna?
17. Teen: yeh **zaroorat** hai, ya sirf EMI chhoti dikh rahi hai isliye?
18. Jo is sale mein sab kuch EMI pe le raha hai, usse yeh **bhejo.**
19. Paisa samjho. Yeh hai **Money Theory.**

## Diwali gifts and tax
*Diwali gift pe tax?* · 18 lines · ~1041 chars

> Facts: Income-tax Act 2025 (from 1 Apr 2026) keeps the gift rules: gifts from relatives exempt; gifts on own marriage exempt; from non-relatives, if the aggregate in a year exceeds Rs 50,000 the whole amount is taxed as other income (money, and movable property such as jewellery/shares by fair value). Employer non-cash gifts/vouchers: perquisite nil up to Rs 15,000 a year (Income-tax Rules 2026, notified 20 Mar 2026; was Rs 5,000). Cash gifts/bonus from employer: salary.

01. Diwali pe gifts. Mithai, sona, cash, **voucher.**
02. Par kuch gifts pe **income tax** bhi lag sakta hai. Kaunse?
03. Pehla rule. **Rishtedaar** se mila gift, kitna bhi bada, **tax-free.**
04. Maa-baap, bhai-behen, pati-patni, saas-sasur. Poori list **law** mein hai.
05. Aur apni **shaadi** pe mila gift bhi tax-free. Kisi se bhi mile.
06. Ab **dost** ya jaan-pehchaan wale.
07. Saal bhar mein inse mile gifts ka total **₹50,000** tak, koi tax nahi.
08. Par total ₹50,000 se upar gaya, toh **poora amount** income. Sirf upar wala hissa nahi.
09. Dosto se ₹60,000 mile? Toh poore **₹60,000** pe aapke slab se tax.
10. Aur yeh sirf cash nahi. **Sona, shares** jaisi cheezein bhi ginti mein.
11. Ab office. Company se mila **voucher ya gift.**
12. Is saal se naya rule: saal mein **₹15,000** tak tax-free. Pehle sirf ₹5,000 tha.
13. Par cash gift ya **Diwali bonus?** Woh salary hai. Pehle rupaye se taxable.
14. Toh kya karein? Bada gift mila, toh kisne diya, kitna, **likh kar** rakho.
15. **Bank transfer** se mile, toh record apne aap ban jaata hai.
16. Aur ITR mein taxable gift **'other income'** mein dikhana mat bhoolo.
17. Jise is Diwali bada gift milne wala hai, usse yeh **bhejo.**
18. Paisa samjho. Yeh hai **Money Theory.**
